## What it does

A patient leaves a voice note and the appointment books itself.

- **Understands the voice note.** OpenAI Whisper transcribes it.
- **Pulls out the details.** GPT extracts the appointment details from the transcript.
- **Books the slot.** The appointment goes straight into Google Calendar.
- **Confirms by text.** Twilio sends the patient an SMS confirmation.
