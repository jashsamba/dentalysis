Luna is privacy-first by design. Every step, from hearing the wake word to speaking the answer, runs on my own machine with no cloud calls.

## What it does

- **Wakes on its name.** Say "Luna" and it starts listening.
- **Knows who is talking.** Speaker recognition tells voices apart.
- **Answers locally.** A local Llama model (llama3.2:3b) replies, and Kokoro turns the reply into natural speech.
- **Controls the laptop.** Volume, apps, and Spotify.
- **Remembers.** Past conversations are embedded for semantic search, so Luna can recall what was said before.
- **Keeps its data fresh.** Apache Airflow pipelines pull financial data, and a live local dashboard shows what is happening.
